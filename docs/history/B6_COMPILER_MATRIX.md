## Why not just compare counts

The M10 version of this matrix passed on `same suite ⇒ same count`. Two runtimes
agreeing on a NUMBER is not evidence they agree on what they ran: a suite can be
renamed, dropped or skipped asymmetrically and the arithmetic still balances. The
rows above compare the sorted set of `classname#method` ids actually executed.
